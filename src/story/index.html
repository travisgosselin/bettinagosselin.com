My story.

I’m Bettina. I write about faith, hope, and the redeeming love of Jesus.

In Anchor of Truth, I share my personal journey through pain toward hope, and the love that changed my life.

My reflections grow out of the life I share with my husband, Travis, and our boys: the ordinary moments, the unexpected challenges, and the reminders of God’s goodness along the way.

The heart behind the words

Why I share my story.

My writing is a place to reflect on faith in the middle of everyday life. Here, I share thoughts on Scripture, gratitude, and remembering God’s faithfulness when life feels uncertain.

I remember being a child in Mexico City when the earth began to shake in 1985. That night, my dad sat with me and spoke about Jesus. Looking back, I can see how those early conversations about faith stayed with me, even through the years when I was far from God.

My story includes seasons of hurt and broken trust. But those chapters are not the whole story. Jesus brought hope into the darkness, and His redeeming love changed my life. That is the heart of Anchor of Truth.

I write because I want to remember what God has done, and to encourage others to look to Him. I do not have a perfect life or all the answers. What I do have is a hope that reaches beyond my circumstances.

Faith in the everyday

Small moments.
Lasting reminders.

Some of my reflections begin with a passage of Scripture. Others begin with a family celebration, a memory, a conversation, or a moment when I am reminded to be thankful.

I have written about the care of our church family, the comfort of prayer, the joy of marriage and motherhood, and the strength God gives when life is uncertain. Through it all, my desire is to point toward Jesus and the light He brings into dark places.
